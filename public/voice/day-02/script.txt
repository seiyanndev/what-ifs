What if the Roman Empire never fell?

In 476 AD, the last emperor in the West was removed from power, and Western Rome broke apart.

The Eastern half, the Byzantine Empire, lasted almost a thousand more years, until 1453.

But imagine Rome holding together.

Europe might never have split into dozens of kingdoms. One empire, one law, one main language: Latin.

Rome already had a simple steam device, built by Hero of Alexandria. Some historians wonder if a stable Rome could have built on ideas like that.

Roman ships might have reached the Americas instead of Spanish ones.

Today, you might be reading this in Latin.
