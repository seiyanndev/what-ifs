What if Magellan had won the Battle of Mactan?

In April 1521, Ferdinand Magellan attacked Mactan, a small island in what's now the Philippines.

Lapulapu and his warriors defeated him. Magellan was killed, and his crew sailed away.

But what if Magellan won?

Spain might have taken control of the islands decades earlier. In real history, colonization didn't start until 1565.

Magellan would have returned a hero, and probably led more expeditions across the Pacific himself.

And Lapulapu, today a national hero, might be a forgotten name.

Today, Lapulapu is a symbol of resistance. One battle made that possible.
