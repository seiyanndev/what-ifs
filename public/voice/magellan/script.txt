What if Magellan never left Spain?

In 1519, Ferdinand Magellan sailed west for the King of Spain. His mission? Find a sea route to the Spice Islands.

His fleet crossed the vast Pacific, starving and exhausted... and in 1521, they reached the Philippines.

On the island of Mactan, one chieftain refused to bow. His name was Lapulapu.

In April 1521, Magellan attacked Mactan... and fell in the shallows.

But what if that voyage never happened at all?

Imagine the king says *no*. Magellan's five ships never leave Seville.

No expedition means no Spanish claim to these islands... at least, not yet.

And Lapulapu? He'd never become a symbol of resistance. Just a datu{da-too}, ruling Mactan in peace.

One voyage that never sailed... and the map of Asia looks completely different. So, what would *you* change?
