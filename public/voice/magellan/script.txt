What if Magellan never left Spain?

In 1519, a Portuguese navigator sailing for Spain set out west, hunting for a sea route to the Spice Islands.

After crossing the vast Pacific, his starving fleet reached the Philippines in 1521.

On the island of Mactan, a chieftain named Lapulapu refused to bow to a foreign king.

In April 1521, Magellan attacked... and fell in the shallows of Mactan.

But what if that voyage never happened at all?

Picture it: the Spanish crown says *no*, and Magellan's five ships rot in Seville's harbor.

Without that expedition, Spain has no reason to claim these islands... at least for now.

Lapulapu would never become a symbol of resistance. He'd just be a datu, ruling Mactan in peace.

One voyage that never sailed... and the whole map of Asia changes. What would *you* change?
