What if the Black Death never happened?

In 1347, a plague swept into Europe. In just a few years, it killed somewhere between a third and half of all Europeans.

The survivors had a strange advantage. Workers were so rare that they could demand higher wages, and the old serf system started to crack.

So what if it *never* came?

Europe stays crowded. Labor stays cheap. Peasants stay tied to the land for longer.

With cheap workers everywhere, there's less reason to invent labor-saving machines.

Some historians think the Renaissance and later the Industrial Revolution might have come later, or looked very different.

A disaster that killed *millions* may also have helped push Europe into the modern world.
