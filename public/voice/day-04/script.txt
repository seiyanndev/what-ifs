What if China had discovered America first?

In 1405, Admiral Zheng{Jung} He{Huh} set sail with one of the largest fleets the world had ever seen. Hundreds of ships, some reported to be several times bigger than Columbus's.

Over seven voyages, his fleet reached Southeast Asia, India, Arabia and East Africa.

Then, after 1433, the Ming court stopped the voyages. China turned inward.

But what if they *kept* sailing east?

A Chinese fleet could have reached the Americas decades before Columbus.

Zheng{Jung} He's{Huhs} voyages were mostly about trade and showing power. First contact might have looked very different.

The Americas might speak Chinese today, and world trade might revolve around the Pacific, not the Atlantic.

One decision to stop sailing changed the world. Would you have kept going?
